Privacy Policy – Floelight

Last updated: September 23, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the game Floelight: Cozy Penguin Puzzle (Android) by Acids Studio.

1. Controller

Acids Studio, Jan Saurer
Email: acidsstudio@gmail.com

2. Data stored on your device

Your game progress (unlocked levels, stars, best scores, hint stock, collected penguin friends, daily puzzle results and streaks) and your settings (volume, language, accessibility) are stored only on your device and are not sent to us. They are deleted when you uninstall the app.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Floelight is free and funded by ads. We use Google AdMob by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland ("Google"). Ads appear occasionally between two levels, and optionally when you choose to watch a video for an extra hint.

Google processes in particular:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it
  • device and app information (e.g. device model, operating system, language, app version)
  • interactions with ads (e.g. shown, clicked, video watched)
  • diagnostic data for error and fraud detection

Consent: Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked on first launch, through a consent dialog (Google User Messaging Platform, certified under the IAB TCF), whether data may be used for personalised ads and for accessing information on the device. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Without consent, only limited, non-personalised ads are served where legally permitted.

Withdrawal: You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in the game under Settings → About & privacy → Privacy choices (ads). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

International transfers: Google may transfer data to Google LLC in the USA. Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (adequacy decision, Art. 45 GDPR); standard contractual clauses apply additionally.

More information: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from apps that use its services

3a. Purchases (Floelight Plus)

The optional purchase "Floelight Plus" is handled entirely by Google Play (Google Ireland Limited). You enter payment details only with Google; we never receive them. The app only learns from Google Play whether Floelight Plus was bought for your Google account and stores this locally on your device. The legal basis is performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Google's privacy policy applies.

3b. Ratings and sharing

Rating: Now and then the app asks through the Google Play rating dialog (In-App Review) whether you enjoy the game. You rate directly on Google Play; we do not learn whether or how you rated.

Sharing: Only when you tap “Share” on the daily puzzle does the Android share menu open with a short result text (no personal data). You decide where to send it; we receive nothing.

4. Permissions

  • Internet / network state – to load ads. The game itself also works offline.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) – for ads, see section 3.
  • Vibrate – optional haptic feedback, can be turned off in the settings.
  • Google Play Billing (com.android.vending.BILLING) – only for the optional purchase, see section 3a.
  • Technical permissions of the ads SDK (e.g. background tasks, wake lock, basic phone state) – so Google can load ads reliably. No access to your phone number, calls, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or GPS location.

5. Children

Floelight is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and to withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. As we do not store any data about you ourselves, please also contact Google regarding data processed by Google.

We update this privacy policy when the app or the legal situation changes. The version published here always applies.
